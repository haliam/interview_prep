# Career Growth Pitch

> **Purpose:** Two versions of your career narrative — a formal written pitch (LinkedIn / CV) and a conversational spoken version (interviews).

---

## Part 1 — Formal Pitch (chronological)

### Proactive Learning — Junior .NET Developer (2019)

I began my career as a Junior .NET at Proactive Learning in 2019, where I built the technical foundation. I worked with EF, front-end features, and fixing bugs in MVC applications, which gave me a solid grounding in both back-end and user experience development.

### Compensa — Mid-Level .NET Engineer (2021)

In 2021, I moved into a Mid-Level .NET Engineer role at Compensa, where I contributed to architecture and performance improvements as part of the development team. I worked on applications using CQRS, helped optimise our main application, supported refactoring of legacy modules, and collaborated on documentation standards to make onboarding and teamwork smoother.

### Inetum — Senior .NET Engineer & Tech Lead (2023–present)

Since 2023, I've been a Senior .NET Engineer and Tech Lead at Inetum, leading a cross-functional squad. I've focused on standardising Agile delivery, improving code quality through clean architecture, and driving innovation with tools like Azure OpenAI and GitHub Copilot. I also work closely with clients to streamline sprint planning and remove blockers, ensuring the team delivers effectively.

### Closing — the evolution

Over time, my work has evolved from hands-on coding to influencing architecture, refining processes, and guiding team practices — all with a focus on delivering better quality, faster results, and stronger user experiences. And always with a strong focus on solutions.

## Part 2 — Conversational Pitch (spoken)

> "I started out as a junior .NET developer, really focused on learning the craft — building features, fixing bugs, and getting comfortable with the full stack. Over time, I moved into a mid-level role where I began shaping how we built things, improving architecture, and making the codebase easier for everyone to work with. Now, as a senior engineer and tech lead, I'm balancing hands-on coding with guiding a squad, designing solutions from scratch, and working closely with clients to make sure what we deliver really fits their needs. It's been a steady climb from writing code to shaping how teams work and how products come to life."
