# 01 — Recruiter Call Script

> **Purpose:** Screening calls (10–15 min). Concise, metric-driven, easy to deliver naturally.

---

## The Script

### 1) Opening — set the tone

**Recruiter:** "Hi, is now a good time to talk?"

**You:**
> "Yes, absolutely. Thanks for reaching out. I am happy to learn more about the role and see whether there is a strong mutual fit."

### 2) Walk me through your experience

**You (60 seconds):**
> "I am a Senior .NET Engineer with 5+ years of experience building scalable backend systems with .NET Core and Azure. In my most recent role at INETUM, I combined hands-on engineering with technical leadership across a 10-engineer squad. I helped improve productivity by 60%, increased code coverage from 35% to 90%, and supported a 98% delivery reliability rate. Before that, I worked on CQRS, performance optimization, and legacy modernization at Compensa, and I started in full-stack .NET development at Proactive Learning."

### 3) Strongest technologies

**Recruiter:** "What technologies are you strongest in?"

**You:**
> "My strongest areas are .NET Core, C#, Azure cloud services, microservices, clean architecture, and CI/CD. I also focus strongly on engineering productivity and delivery quality, not just feature output."

### 4) What you want next

**Recruiter:** "What are you looking for in your next role?"

**You:**
> "I am looking for a role where I can combine hands-on .NET development with technical leadership, contribute to cloud-native architecture decisions, and help improve engineering standards and delivery performance."

### 5) Why leaving current role

**Recruiter:** "Why are you leaving your current role?"

**You:**
> "I am looking for a more product-led, cloud-focused environment where I can have greater impact on architecture, scalability, and engineering culture."

### 6) Notice period

**Recruiter:** "What is your notice period?"

**You:**
> "I can support a smooth handover and align on a practical start date with the right opportunity."

### 7) UK work authorization and sponsorship

**Recruiter:** "Do you have the right to work in the UK?"

**You:**
> "I am currently based in the UK and open to remote or hybrid roles. I require Skilled Worker sponsorship, so I am focusing on employers who already sponsor. Many companies I have spoken with are already licensed sponsors, so I mainly focus on roles where sponsorship is already part of the hiring process. If sponsorship is available for this role, I would be very happy to proceed."

### 8) Salary expectations

**Recruiter:** "What are your salary expectations?"

**You:**
> "Based on London market levels and the ownership I bring, I am targeting GBP 90k–100k base, depending on scope and total package."

💡 *For the full negotiation strategy and scripts, see [04_salary_negotiation.md](04_salary_negotiation.md).*

### 9) Target company profile

**Recruiter:** "What type of companies are you targeting?"

**You:**
> "I am most interested in product-driven environments such as fintech or SaaS, where engineering quality, cloud-native design, and continuous improvement are core priorities."

### 10) Strong close

**You:**
> "Thanks again for the conversation. This sounds aligned from my side. Could you share the job description, confirmed salary band, sponsorship status, and next interview steps with expected timeline?"

---

## Recruiter Qualification Questions

Ask these early:

1. Is visa sponsorship available for this role?
2. Is this a new role or a backfill?
3. What is the team structure and reporting line?
4. What are the interview stages and expected timeline?
5. What salary band is already approved for this role?

---

## 30-Second Elevator Pitch

> "I am a Senior .NET Engineer focused on building scalable, cloud-native systems with .NET Core and Azure. I bring a mix of hands-on backend engineering and technical leadership, with measurable impact in productivity, quality, and delivery reliability. I am now looking for a high-impact senior role in a product-led environment."

---

## Delivery Tips

💡 **Tips:**

- Keep answers concise and conversational.
- Lead with measurable outcomes.
- Keep tone confident, direct, and positive.
- Avoid long explanations unless asked.
- Close every call with clear next-step questions.
