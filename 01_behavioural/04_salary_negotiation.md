## Part 2 — Negotiation Scripts

A polished, natural-sounding script tailored to your CV and UK communication style.

### 📞 A) First recruiter call — set your anchor

**Recruiter:** "What salary are you looking for?"

**You:**
> "At this stage I'm mainly focused on understanding the role and expectations.
> Based on similar Senior .NET roles in London, I'm targeting a range of **£90k to £100k**, depending on scope, ownership, and the technical challenges involved."

**Why this works:** You anchor high, stay flexible, and sound informed — not aggressive.

### 🔄 A2) Alternative — deflect to their range first

If you'd rather they anchor first:

**You:**
> "What is the salary range for this role? What skills and experience separate those that would be at £60k from those that would be at £70k?"

**Then:**
> "If I match all the skills you are asking for, I'm at the top of that range."

**Why this works:** You make them reveal their band and justify the top of it with their own criteria.

### 🧑‍💼 B) During technical / hiring manager interviews

**You:**
> "Given the responsibilities we've discussed — particularly around microservices, Azure, and technical leadership — I'd expect compensation in the **upper end of the £90k–£100k range**.
>
> I'm confident I can deliver the level of impact that justifies that, especially considering my track record improving delivery performance, code quality, and engineering productivity."

### 📩 C) When the offer comes in lower than expected

**Them:** "We can offer £85k."

**You:**
> "Thank you, I appreciate the offer.
> Based on my experience leading teams, improving delivery efficiency by 60–98%, and building scalable Azure-based solutions, I was expecting something closer to **£100k**.
> Is there flexibility to bring the base salary closer to that figure?"

### 🧱 D) If they say the salary cannot increase

**You:**
> "I understand. If the base salary is fixed, is there flexibility in other parts of the package — such as bonus, equity, training budget, or a 6-month performance-based salary review?"

### 🤝 E) Closing the negotiation

**You:**
> "I'm excited about the role and confident I can deliver strong results quickly.
> If we can align the package around **£X**, I'm happy to move forward."
