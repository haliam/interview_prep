# Remote Job Platforms

> **Purpose:** Curated list of remote job platforms, tech-specialized aggregators, and companies that hire remotely.

---

## General Platforms

- **FlexJobs** → Filtered remote jobs, zero scams.
- **We Work Remotely** → Top startups and technology.
- **Remote.co** → Genuine remote-first companies.
- **Himalayas** → Full transparency: salaries, culture, and tech stack.
- **Jobgether** → Filter by country, language, and flexibility.
- **SkipTheDrive** → From marketing to legal: a bit of everything.
- **Virtual Vocations** → Very strong in the US, but with global roles.

---

## Tech & Freelance Sector

- **Arc.dev** → If you're a dev, this is your league.
- **RemoteOK** → All the new jobs appear here first.
- **Wellfound (AngelList)** → Startups looking for candidates with initiative.
- **4 Day Week** → Yes, 4-day work weeks!
- **Contra** → Visual, direct, and commission-free.
- **Hubstaff Talent** → Freelance tech without intermediaries.

---

## Aggregators

- **Remotive** → Community + real offers.
- **DailyRemote** → Updated every day.
- **Jobspresso** → Tech, marketing, and support.
- **Working Nomads** → If you dream of working from anywhere.
- **Career Vault** → Clean, useful, and categorized search engine.

---

## Companies That Hire Remotely

- **Automattic** → WordPress and WooCommerce, 100% remote.
- **GitLab** → No offices. No excuses.
- **Hotjar** → Real flexibility, remote team across Europe.
- **Buffer** → Communication, distributed team, and strong culture.
- **Typeform** → Design and UX from Barcelona to the world.
- **Moodle** → Global education, based in Spain.
- **Qonto and N26** → European fintechs with distributed teams.

---

## Tips for Landing a Remote Job

💡 **Tips:**

- Customize your CV and apply directly on the company's website.
- Talk to employees on LinkedIn (not just HR).
- Use Notion or Teal HQ to manage your applications.
- Work on your personal brand: it will open doors you can't even imagine.
