# Step-by-Step — Skilled Worker Visa Application

> **Purpose:** The 8 steps to apply for a UK Skilled Worker visa, from Certificate of Sponsorship to Biometric Residence Permit.

---

## Steps

### 1) Obtain a Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS)

Secure a CoS from your UK employer. This document is crucial for your visa application.

### 2) Complete the Online Application Form

Fill out the application form on the UK government's official website, providing detailed personal and employment information.

### 3) Pay the Application Fee

Submit the applicable visa application fee, which varies based on the visa duration and whether you're applying from inside or outside the UK.

### 4) Schedule a Biometric Appointment

After submitting your application, schedule an appointment at a visa application center to provide your fingerprints and photograph.

### 5) Submit Supporting Documents

Gather and submit all necessary documents, including your passport, CoS, proof of English proficiency, and evidence of maintenance funds.

### 6) Attend a Visa Interview (if required)

You may be required to attend an interview to verify the information in your application.

### 7) Wait for a Decision

After completing all steps, wait for a decision on your application. Processing times can vary, but you can track your application online.

### 8) Collect Your Biometric Residence Permit (BRP)

If approved, you'll receive a BRP, which serves as proof of your right to stay in the UK.
