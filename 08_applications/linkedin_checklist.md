# LinkedIn Checklist & Weekly Strategy

> **Purpose:** Final profile checklist, weekly activity strategy, and ready-to-send message templates for UK recruiter outreach.

---

## Part 1 — Final Checklist

### Profile

- [ ] Professional photo
- [ ] Custom banner
- [ ] Keyword-optimized headline
- [ ] Achievement-focused About section (in English)
- [ ] Experience with metrics and tech stack
- [ ] Complete and well-ordered skills
- [ ] Endorsements from colleagues

### Settings

- [ ] Location set to the UK
- [ ] Open to Work enabled
- [ ] Profile 100% in English

### Activity

- [ ] Publish 2–3 posts per week
- [ ] Comment on posts from UK tech companies
- [ ] Connect with 10 recruiters per week

### Premium

- [ ] Use Job Insights
- [ ] Send 3–5 InMails weekly
- [ ] Check who viewed your profile

---

## Part 2 — Weekly Strategy

### Monday — Technical Post

Share a short post about:

- An architecture pattern (CQRS, Clean Architecture)
- A performance improvement
- A reflection on .NET 8

### Tuesday — Networking

- Connect with 10 UK recruiters
- Comment on 5 posts from UK tech companies
- Send 2 InMails

### Wednesday — Value Post

Examples:

- "3 things I learned while leading a .NET team"
- "How I increased test coverage from 35% to 90%"

### Thursday — Optimization

- Review Premium Job Insights
- Adjust your skills based on job descriptions
- Apply to 10 roles with a tailored CV

### Friday — Light Activity

- Share an article about Azure or .NET
- Comment on posts from other developers
- Thank new connections

### Saturday/Sunday — Preparation

- Review your profile
- Prepare content for the upcoming week
- Research UK companies hiring .NET developers

---

## Part 3 — Message Templates

### Connect

> "Hi [Name], I'm a Senior .NET Engineer specialising in C#, .NET 8, Azure, and microservices. I'm currently exploring opportunities in the UK market and would love to connect with you."

### InMail

> "Hi [Name], I hope you're doing well. I'm a Senior .NET Engineer with 5+ years of experience in C#, .NET 8, ASP.NET Core, Azure, and microservices. I've led teams, improved delivery pipelines, and delivered scalable backend solutions for enterprise clients.
>
> I'm very interested in roles such as Senior .NET Developer or Backend Engineer in the UK. If you think my profile could be a good fit for any of your current or upcoming positions, I'd be happy to share more details.
>
> Thanks for your time."
