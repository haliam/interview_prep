# 30-Day Plan to Get Interviews in the UK

> **Purpose:** Day-by-day LinkedIn and applications plan to turn your profile into interview opportunities in 30 days.

---

## Week 1 — Profile Transformation (Visibility Boost)

🎯 **Goal:** Make your profile irresistible to UK recruiters.

- [ ] **Day 1:** Update headline (SEO + seniority)
- [ ] **Day 2:** Rewrite About section (achievement-focused)
- [ ] **Day 3:** Rewrite experience with metrics + tech stack
- [ ] **Day 4:** Reorder skills (C#, .NET 8, Azure first)
- [ ] **Day 5:** Add banner + professional photo
- [ ] **Day 6:** Set location to UK + Open to Work
- [ ] **Day 7:** Review everything for clarity and consistency

---

## Week 2 — Market Positioning & Networking

🎯 **Goal:** Enter the UK recruiter ecosystem.

- [ ] **Day 8:** Identify 20 target companies in the UK
- [ ] **Day 9:** Save 20–30 relevant job posts (Premium feature)
- [ ] **Day 10:** Connect with 20 UK recruiters
- [ ] **Day 11:** Join 10 UK tech groups
- [ ] **Day 12:** Comment on 10 posts from UK companies
- [ ] **Day 13:** Send 3–5 InMails to recruiters
- [ ] **Day 14:** Publish a technical post (.NET, Azure, architecture)

---

## Week 3 — Applications & Outreach

🎯 **Goal:** Start generating interview opportunities.

- [ ] **Day 15:** Apply to 10 tailored roles
- [ ] **Day 16:** Send follow-up messages to recruiters
- [ ] **Day 17:** Practice technical interviews (.NET, C#, Azure)
- [ ] **Day 18:** Publish a value post ("How I improved X by Y%")
- [ ] **Day 19:** Apply to 10 more roles
- [ ] **Day 20:** Ask for referrals from UK connections
- [ ] **Day 21:** Review Premium Job Insights and adjust skills

---

## Week 4 — Conversion to Interviews

🎯 **Goal:** Turn visibility + applications into interviews.

- [ ] **Day 22:** Follow up on all applications
- [ ] **Day 23:** Do mock interviews (technical + behavioural)
- [ ] **Day 24:** Publish a post about leadership or delivery impact
- [ ] **Day 25:** Connect with 20 more UK professionals
- [ ] **Day 26:** Apply to 10 additional roles
- [ ] **Day 27:** Review your CV and LinkedIn for consistency
- [ ] **Day 28:** Prepare STAR stories for interviews
- [ ] **Day 29:** Review progress and refine strategy
- [ ] **Day 30:** Send a final round of InMails + applications

---

## Expected Results After 30 Days

If you follow this plan consistently, you can normally expect:

- 3–6 technical interviews
- 1–2 interviews with hiring managers
- 50–100 new UK connections
- Visibility increase x3–x5
- Replies to InMails and recruiters contacting you directly
