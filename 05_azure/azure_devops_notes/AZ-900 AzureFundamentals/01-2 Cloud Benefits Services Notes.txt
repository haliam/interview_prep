Describe the benefits of using cloud services
-------------------------------------------------------

	When building or deploying a cloud application, two of the biggest considerations are uptime (or availability) and the ability to handle demand (or scale).
	SLAs = Service-level agreements (Formal agreement between service provider and customer)


	Describe the benefits of high availability and scalability in the cloud
	------------------------------------------------------------------------------
	
	Availability = up time (Time the service is available) vs down (Time the service is unavailable) 

	Service 99% SLA vs Service 99,9% SLA 
	99% 1.6 h unavailable per week or 7h per month
	99,9% 10 min unavailable per week or 43 minutes per month
	
	Scalability in Cloud Computing:

	Scalability in cloud computing is the ability to adjust resources as demand changes, ensuring efficient performance without overspending.

	Types of Scaling:

	Vertical Scaling: Increases or decreases the capacity of a single resource. For instance, adding more CPU or RAM to a virtual machine (scale up) or reducing them if not needed (scale down).
	Horizontal Scaling: Adds or removes resource instances. For example, during high traffic, more virtual machines are added (scale out); when demand drops, machines are reduced (scale in).

	Scalability enables seamless handling of peak loads while controlling costs.
	
	
	
	Describe the benefits of reliability and predictability in the cloud
	------------------------------------------------------------------------------
	Reliability: 
	The cloud is designed to keep things running even if one part fails. 
	Resources are spread globally, so if one region has an issue, others keep working. Some systems even switch regions automatically to stay reliable.

	Predictability:
	Performance Predictability: Cloud services support steady performance with features like autoscaling, load balancing, and high availability. 
								These features ensure resources automatically adjust to meet demand and distribute traffic evenly, keeping user experiences positive.
								
	Cost Predictability: Cloud cost management tools allow real-time tracking and analysis, helping predict expenses accurately. 
						 Tools like Azure’s Pricing Calculator and Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) assist in planning and controlling cloud spend effectively.
		
				
	
	Describe the benefits of security and governance in the cloud
	------------------------------------------------------------------------------
	Cloud features help ensure that your deployed resources meet corporate and regulatory standards. 
	You can use templates to ensure consistency, and cloud-based auditing flags any resources that are out of compliance, 
	offering solutions for remediation. Updates to standards can also be easily applied to existing resources.

	Security:

	Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS): Gives you control over security, including managing patches and maintenance yourself.
	Platform as a Service (PaaS) & Software as a Service (SaaS): These models handle security tasks like patches and maintenance for you.
	
	Cloud providers are also equipped to handle security threats, like Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks, making your infrastructure more secure.
	By establishing strong governance from the start, you can keep your cloud environment secure, updated, and compliant.
	
	
	Describe the benefits of manageability in the cloud
	------------------------------------------------------------------------------
	A major benefit of cloud computing is the manageability options:
	
	Management of the cloud (managing your cloud resources)

	Automatically scale resource deployment based on need.
	Deploy resources based on a preconfigured template, removing the need for manual configuration.
	Monitor the health of resources and automatically replace failing resources.
	Receive automatic alerts based on configured metrics, so you’re aware of performance in real time.
	
	Management in the cloud (manage your cloud environment and resources)

	Through a web portal.
	Using a command line interface.
	Using APIs.
	Using PowerShell.
