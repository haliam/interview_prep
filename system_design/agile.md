# Agile & Scrum

> **Purpose:** Quick-reference summary of Scrum roles, artifacts, events, and team characteristics for interview preparation.

## Scrum Roles

### Product Owner

- Responsible for maximizing the return on investment (ROI) of the development effort.
- Responsible for the product vision.
- Constantly re-prioritizes the Product Backlog.

### ScrumMaster

- Helps remove impediments.
- Creates an environment that fosters team self-organization.
- Captures empirical data to adjust forecasts.
- Protects the team from external interference and distractions.

### Scrum Development Team

- Cross-functional: includes members with testing skills and often others not traditionally called developers (business analysts, domain experts, etc.).
- Self-organizing / self-managing, with no externally assigned roles.
- Negotiates commitments with the Product Owner, one Sprint at a time.
