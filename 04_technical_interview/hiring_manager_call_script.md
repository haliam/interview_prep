# 🎯 Hiring-Manager Call Script

> **Purpose:** Script for the hiring-manager interview — confident, metric-driven answers that position you as a senior engineer with leadership and architectural thinking.

---

## 1. Opening — Set a Confident, Collaborative Tone

**Hiring Manager:** "Great to meet you. Let's start with your background."

**You:**
> "Great to meet you as well. I'm happy to walk you through my experience and understand more about the technical challenges your team is solving."

**Why this works:** This shows maturity and partnership.

---

## 2. "Tell me about your experience."

**You:**
> "I've spent the last five years building scalable backend systems using .NET Core, Azure, and clean architecture. In my current role as a Senior .NET Engineer and Tech Lead at INETUM, I focus on improving engineering performance and system reliability. I introduced clean architecture, raised code coverage from 35% to 90%, and improved team productivity by 60% through automation and better development practices. I also led a squad of ten developers, coordinated sprint refinement, and delivered Azure-based solutions including an AI avatar using Azure OpenAI. Before that, I worked on CQRS, performance optimisation, and legacy refactoring at Compensa."

**Why this works:** This positions you as a senior engineer with leadership and architectural thinking.

---

## 3. "What kind of systems have you worked on?"

**You:**
> "Mainly distributed backend systems built with .NET Core, REST APIs, microservices, and Azure services. I've worked on systems where scalability, maintainability, and delivery consistency were key priorities. I tend to apply clean architecture, domain-driven thinking, and strong testing practices to keep systems stable and easy to evolve."

---

## 4. "How do you approach architecture and design?"

**You:**
> "I start by understanding the domain and the constraints — performance, scalability, data flow, and integration points. From there, I design using clean architecture principles, clear boundaries, and separation of concerns. I'm a strong believer in testability and maintainability, so I avoid over-engineering and focus on patterns that bring long-term value."

**Why this works:** This shows architectural maturity.

---

## 5. "How do you ensure code quality?"

**You:**
> "I combine clean architecture, SOLID principles, automated testing, and CI/CD. In my current team, these practices increased code coverage from 35% to 90% and reduced defects significantly. I also encourage pair programming, code reviews, and shared ownership."

---

## 6. "Tell me about a technical challenge you solved."

**You:**
> "One example was building an AI avatar using Azure OpenAI for MWC. The challenge was performance under high load. I designed an Azure-based architecture using Functions, App Services, and caching strategies. This reduced user wait times by 30% and handled traffic reliably during live demos."

---

## 7. "How do you work with cloud technologies?"

**You:**
> "I've deployed and maintained services using Azure App Services, Functions, Storage, and Azure DevOps pipelines. I'm comfortable designing cloud-native architectures, optimising cost and performance, and ensuring reliability through monitoring and automation."

---

## 8. "How do you collaborate with teams?"

**You:**
> "I keep communication clear, align early with stakeholders, and help the team break down work into manageable, high-impact tasks. As a Tech Lead, I focused on removing blockers, mentoring developers, and improving delivery flow. This helped the team reach a 98% delivery rate."

---

## 9. "What are you looking for in your next role?"

**You:**
> "I'm looking for a role where I can contribute to cloud-native architecture, work on scalable systems, and help elevate engineering practices. I enjoy combining hands-on development with technical leadership and continuous improvement."

---

## 10. Closing — Leave a Strong Technical Impression

**You:**
> "Thanks for the conversation. I'd be happy to go deeper into architecture, cloud, or any part of my experience in the next stage. Let me know what the team is solving right now — I'd love to understand where I can add the most value."

**Why this works:** This shows ownership, curiosity, and seniority.
