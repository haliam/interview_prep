# Top 10 Questions to Ask (Company)

> **Purpose:** Questions to ask the interviewer about the company — technical stack, organisation & workflow, and HR & culture — to evaluate a senior .NET role.

---

## **🔑 Top 10 Questions to Ask**

1.  Which version of .NET and Visual Studio are you currently working with?

2.  What languages, frameworks, and architectural patterns define your applications?

3.  How do you handle version control and CI/CD pipelines?

4.  Are you mainly maintaining legacy systems, migrating, or building new projects?

5.  What development methodology do you follow (Agile, Scrum, Kanban)?

6.  How are tasks assigned within sprints, and how long are your sprint cycles?

7.  What roles make up the development team I’d be working with?

8.  What would my first two weeks look like in terms of onboarding and responsibilities?

9.  What key contributions do you expect from someone stepping into this role?

10. What skills are expected of someone positioned at the top of the salary range?

---

## **🔧 Technical Stack Extended**

- Which version of Visual Studio and .NET are you currently working with?

- What version control system do you use (e.g., Git, TFS), and how is branching/merging typically managed?

- What languages and frameworks make up the core of your codebase?

- Do your projects include unit tests, and which testing frameworks are standard (MSTest, NUnit, xUnit)?

- How is test coverage and quality assurance handled across projects?

- Are your applications primarily monolithic, or do you use microservices or other distributed architectures?

- Are you migrating legacy systems, building new ones, or balancing both?

- What architectural patterns (e.g., CQRS, Clean Architecture, Event‑Driven) are most common in your modern applications?

- How do you approach CI/CD and deployment pipelines?

---

## **📈 Organisation & Workflow**

- Do you follow Agile or another methodology, and how is it adapted to your teams?

- What is the typical sprint length, and how are tasks assigned or prioritized?

- Do developers usually focus on a single project or contribute across multiple applications?

- What roles make up the development team (Architect, Scrum Master, Product Owner, QA, etc.)?

- How is the balance between bug fixing/incidents and new feature development managed?

- Are there upcoming greenfield projects or major initiatives on the roadmap?

- How do teams collaborate with product and business stakeholders?

---

## **👥 HR & Company Culture**

- How many vacation days and flexible working arrangements (e.g., summer hours) are offered?

- How is salary structured (12 vs. 14 payments per year)?

- What are the standard working hours, and how flexible are they?

- Is the role currently remote, hybrid, or on‑site? What are the long‑term plans?

- What benefits are available (transport allowance, meal vouchers, training budget, health insurance, gym membership, etc.)?

- How large is the team I’d be joining, and how is it structured?

- What would my first two weeks look like in terms of onboarding and responsibilities?

- Which application or project would I start with?

- What are the key contributions you expect from someone in this role?
